**Durable Bank** gives you your own account, named by your IP address, with $100. Send money to a stranger and watch the Transfer saga move it between Durable Objects: through review, the clearing house every visitor shares, the receiver's account, and back to yours if they bounce it. Send to `bob` to see a refund: his account starts frozen.

[Open it full screen](/bank/). Everyone shares one clearing house, so with other visitors around your transfer waits its turn.

| Object | One per | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Account/<your IP>` | visitor | Holds the balance and sets money aside when you send a transfer. A frozen account bounces money sent to it. |
| `Transfer/<id>` | transfer | The saga: `requested → review? → clearing → crediting → settling → completed`, or `→ refunding → refunded`. |
| `Bank/global` | site | The clearing house. It clears one transfer every 2.5 s. |

Transfers of $100 or more wait 12 s in `review`, on an `after` timer that survives eviction. Every hop between objects waits 700 ms so you can see it travel. The source is in [`examples/bank`](https://github.com/ericclemmons/durable-machines/tree/main/examples/bank).
